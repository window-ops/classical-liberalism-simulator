# Classical Liberalism Simulator

This simulator illustrates the economic and social impacts of classical liberalism over a 50-year period, with the first 10 years representing the pre-industrial era. Classical liberalism, which spans the remaining 40 years, is an economic and political philosophy that emphasizes individual rights, private property, free markets, and limited government intervention.

The simulator visualizes GDP, poverty rates, quality of life, inequality, and economic mobility, highlighting the phases of growth, success, and decline.
